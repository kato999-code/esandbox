Privacy Policy

← eSandbox.me

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short

This site does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no login, no newsletter, no shop, no advertising and no analytics. There is no backend and no database — the site is a set of static files served by Cloudflare. Nothing you do here is sent to me.

What the site is

eSandbox.me is a personal sandbox: a few playable games and some writing, published in the open. I run no server of my own that could store information about you.

The pages are served by Cloudflare, which may keep standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, request time, user agent) for security and abuse prevention. That is the only thing any part of this site can see.

The games, specifically

Both games run entirely in your browser, and neither sends anything anywhere.

  • Neon Joust stores nothing on your device.
  • Chess Poker stores a single preference in your browser's local storage: whether the board is shown flipped. That is the only thing either game saves. No scores, no bankrolls and no game progress are stored — they live in memory and are gone when you close the page.

You can remove that preference at any time by clearing your browser's data for this site.

Third-party assets

The animation libraries this site uses (GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis) are hosted from this site's own files, not from a third-party CDN. No third-party script runs on these pages, and no data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Personal automation tooling

Separately from this website, I operate a private automation assistant used only by me. Where it connects to Google services — Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive — access is granted to my own account through Google OAuth, is used solely to read and organise my own data on my own behalf, and is never shared, sold or transferred to any third party. No other user's Google data is accessed, and credentials are stored on hardware I control. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services — Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
