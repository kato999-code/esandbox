Summary

This site collects no personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no accounts. If you play a game, everything happens in your browser.

This website

esandbox.me is a static website. No information you enter is transmitted to or stored by me. Served through Cloudflare, which may keep standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, request time, user agent) for security and abuse prevention.

Games

The games on this site run entirely in your browser. Any scores, settings or progress are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent anywhere.

Personal automation tooling

I operate a private, personal automation assistant used only by me. Where it connects to Google services — Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive — access is granted to my own account through Google OAuth, is used solely to read and organise my own data on my own behalf, and is never shared, sold or transferred to any third party.

No other user's Google data is accessed. Credentials are stored locally on hardware I control. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services — Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
