NEON JOUST

insert coin Enter the arena →
  1. Rule 1

    Perpetual energy walls. Restitution 1.0, zero friction, no deceleration — nothing in the arena ever slows down on its own.

  2. Rule 2

    Combat-driven momentum injection. Damage boosts velocity, so a losing fighter is also the fastest one on the field.

  3. Rule 3

    Weapon clash triggers hit-stop for 4–6 frames, then both bodies bounce apart and their angular spin inverts.
